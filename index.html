About

Driven by Curiosity, Built on Fundamentals

I'm passionate about becoming a well-rounded engineer who bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application. From designing precision components for space missions to building autonomous systems from scratch, I thrive on challenges that demand both deep technical understanding and creative problem-solving.

What excites me most is learning—whether it's mastering new programming paradigms, understanding complex control systems, or iterating through dozens of prototypes to find the optimal solution. I approach each project with intellectual curiosity and a commitment to fundamental engineering principles, believing that strong fundamentals unlock innovative solutions.

Beyond engineering, I'm committed to personal growth and balance. My experience as a varsity swimmer at Princeton taught me discipline, time management, and the value of persistent effort—qualities that directly translate to my engineering work. I'm always seeking opportunities to expand my skill set and contribute to meaningful projects that push the boundaries of what's possible.
